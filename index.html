About

Practical IT support, growing into network operations

I completed an NCV in Information Technology and Computer Science (NQF Level 4) and then spent 20 months as an IT Technician at Ekurhuleni East TVET College, supporting staff and students and working on a live campus network: switches, access points, structured cabling and day-to-day fault finding.

Today I work in fibre customer support, where I check connectivity, work through ONT and router indicators, and escalate faults to technical teams with clear notes. The same habits carry across both roles: isolate the problem, document it properly, and keep the person affected informed.

I have completed the first CCNA course (Introduction to Networks) and I am working towards the full CCNA certification. I am looking for an IT support, NOC or junior network role where I can keep building on that foundation.
